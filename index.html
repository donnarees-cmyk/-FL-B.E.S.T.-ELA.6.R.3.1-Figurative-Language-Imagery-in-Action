Poem: "The Storm Over Lake Okeechobee"

By Mrs. Rees • Click any highlighted line to hear my teaching analysis!

The lake was a mirror, serene and still,

Reflecting the gold on the pine-covered hill.

The quiet wind whispered a secret so low,

Like butter smooth sunbeams that melted below.

Then dark clouds charged in like wild horses at war,

And thunder growled loud with a terrible roar!

Rain lashed at the palm trees with millions of spears,

Until the sky wept all its stormy blue tears.

Figurative Language Matrix

Metaphor (Direct Comparison)

Compares two unlike things directly WITHOUT using 'like' or 'as'.

Ex: "The lake was a mirror..."

Simile (Comparison with 'Like' or 'As')

Compares two unlike things using 'like' or 'as'.

Ex: "Clouds charged in like wild horses..."

Personification (Human Traits to Non-Human)

Gives human feelings, actions, or abilities to objects/nature.

Ex: "The quiet wind whispered a secret..."

Hyperbole (Extreme Exaggeration)

Exaggerates heavily for emphasis or dramatic effect.

Ex: "Rain lashed with millions of spears..."
